Good to know

A few answers,
before you ask.

How do I get started?

Choose Plan a visit to prepare a short contact request. This static template creates a draft for you to review; it does not send it or make an appointment.

Can I choose a time for my visit?

You can include a morning or afternoon contact preference. It is only a preference, not an available appointment slot. A real practice would confirm dates and times directly.

What should I include in my request?

Only your name, email and general visit preference. Please don't enter symptoms, medical records or other sensitive health information. There is no secure patient portal in this demo.

Is Aster Care a real medical practice?

No. Aster Care is a fictional demonstration identity for this free HTML template. The people in the stock photographs are not presented as its staff or patients. Services, hours and location are examples, not real medical services.
